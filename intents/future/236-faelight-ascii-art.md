Every session the forest greets you differently.
Not a splash screen. A moment of presence.
A different piece of forest ASCII art on fsh startup.
Seasonal. Contextual. Occasionally philosophical.
On fsh session start: displays one piece of ASCII art
Art rotates: daily seed so same day = same art
Themes: forest trees, faelight glyphs, Friday watching
Tied to forest state: high health = flourishing tree, low health = bare branches
Toggleable: FAELIGHT_ASCII=0 to disable
20+ pieces minimum:
- Forest scenes (deep woods, single tree, roots)
- Friday watching (eyes in the forest)
- Faelight glyphs (the visual identity)
- Seasonal (spring bloom, summer full, autumn falling, winter bare)
- Milestone art (appears on special commit counts)
⬜ 20+ ASCII art pieces created and stored in assets/
⬜ Daily rotation working (date-seeded selection)
⬜ Health-aware: flourishing vs bare based on health%
⬜ Displayed on fsh startup (before prompt)
⬜ Toggleable via config.fsh
⬜ Milestone art triggers on 500, 1000, 2000 commits

Now you can see it watching back." 🌲
