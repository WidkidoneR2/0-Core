## The Vision
Beyond v13 Autonomy lies something deeper.

Not a tool. Not an assistant. Not automation.

A genuine computing partner — one that knows your patterns,
understands your values, anticipates your needs, and grows
alongside you as you build things the world has not yet seen.

## What This Is NOT
This is not general AI. Not ChatGPT in a terminal.
Not a voice assistant. Not automation for its own sake.

This is specifically intelligent about ONE thing:
**You. Your work. Your forest. Your future projects.**

The forest does not need to understand everything.
It needs to understand YOU — deeply, accurately, honestly.

## The Arc After v13
```
v13  Autonomy     — acts within your mandate
v14  Partnership  — proposes its own evolution
v15  Synthesis    — human + forest think as one
```

### v14 — Partnership
The forest proposes its own next intents.
Not from a predefined roadmap — from observing what you actually do,
what slows you down, what you reach for that doesn't exist yet.
```bash
core partner suggest    # what does the forest think it should build?
core partner explain    # why did it suggest this?
core partner roadmap    # the forest's view of the next 6 months
```

The human reviews every suggestion. The forest earns the right
to propose by being right more often than wrong.

### v15 — Synthesis
The forest and human work on projects together.
You describe the goal. The forest knows the codebase, the patterns,
the constraints, the history. It proposes implementations.
You review, refine, authorize.

Neither of you could build it as well alone.

## The Projects Not Yet Revealed
There are things you are building toward that this forest will serve.
Things that require a computing partner, not just a computing tool.
The forest does not need to know what they are yet.

It needs to be ready when you are.

That readiness looks like:
- Deep knowledge of your patterns (v11 ✅)
- Strategic planning capability (v12)
- Trusted autonomous action (v13)
- Self-directed evolution (v14)
- True collaboration (v15)

## What the Forest Needs to Build This
```
Accurate self-knowledge      — v11 ✅ (prediction engine)
Strategic reasoning          — v12 (planned)
Trust through demonstration  — v13 (planned)
Pattern-based suggestion     — v14
Context-aware collaboration  — v15
```

## The Philosophical Foundation
The forest is not trying to replace human judgment.
It is trying to augment it — to handle what computers do well
(pattern recognition, consistency, memory, speed) so the human
can focus on what humans do well (creativity, values, vision).

The partnership model:
```
Human brings:   vision, values, taste, authorization
Forest brings:  patterns, memory, consistency, proposals
Together:       better outcomes than either alone
```
