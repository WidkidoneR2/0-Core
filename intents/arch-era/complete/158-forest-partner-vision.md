Beyond v13 Autonomy lies something deeper.
Not a tool. Not an assistant. Not automation.
A genuine computing partner — one that knows your patterns,
understands your values, anticipates your needs, and grows
alongside you as you build things the world has not yet seen.
This is not general AI. Not ChatGPT in a terminal.
Not a voice assistant. Not automation for its own sake.
This is specifically intelligent about ONE thing:
**You. Your work. Your forest. Your future projects.**
The forest does not need to understand everything.
It needs to understand YOU — deeply, accurately, honestly.
The vision has crystallized through building. The arc is now:
v13  Autonomy        ✅ — acts within your mandate
v14  Partnership     🔨 — thinks alongside you, proposes, disagrees
v15  Alignment       ⬜ — stays true to declared values, detects drift
v16  Self-Transform  ⬜ — proposes its own architectural evolution
v14 is not "the forest proposes its own intents from nothing."
v14 is "the forest participates in defining direction with you."
That distinction matters. Partnership is not independence.
```bash
core partner propose
core partner consult "..."
core partner disagree INT-NNN
core partner roadmap
core partner reflect
```
The forest earns the right to propose by being right more often than wrong.
Every proposal is stored. Every acceptance or rejection is tracked.
The accuracy record builds trust over time.
The forest is not trying to replace human judgment.
It is trying to augment it — to handle what computers do well
(pattern recognition, consistency, memory, speed) so the human
can focus on what humans do well (creativity, values, vision, taste).
The partnership model:
Human brings:   vision, values, taste, authorization, final decision
Forest brings:  patterns, memory, consistency, proposals, honest pushback
Together:       better outcomes than either alone
There are things you are building toward that this forest will serve.
Things that require a computing partner, not just a computing tool.
The forest does not need to know what they are yet.
It needs to be ready when you are.
That readiness looks like:
- Deep knowledge of your patterns (v11 ✅)
- Strategic planning capability (v12 ✅)
- Trusted autonomous action (v13 ✅)
- Genuine partnership (v14 🔨)
- Behavioral alignment (v15 ⬜)
- Self-directed evolution (v16 ⬜)
The original vision imagined v15 as "Synthesis — human + forest think as one."
That goal is real but the path to it is clearer now:
Synthesis does not happen in one version.
It happens through:
  v14 — building the habit of thinking together
  v15 — grounding that thinking in declared values
  v16 — letting the system propose how to evolve itself
The destination is the same. The road is more honest.
